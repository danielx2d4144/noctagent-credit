## Programme description (verbatim)
"Add trust and accountability to the AI agents you already have."
"Calling all EVM and Solana builders. Your agent already acts for people: signing up, paying, dealing with other agents. What it can't do yet is prove that a verified human is accountable for it. With Concordium, the person behind the agent verifies once, and the agent carries a badge that agents and services on over 200 chains can check, with zero-knowledge proofs keeping that person's data private. Verify once. Prove everywhere. Reveal nothing."
"The Concordium Trusted Agents Hackathon is a free, six-week online programme for AI developers. Start with three weeks of live workshops with the Concordium team, then spend three weeks building on testnet with office hours twice a week. You don't need any Concordium or blockchain experience. If your agent runs on Ethereum, Solana or one of over 200 supported chains, this is for you."

## How it works (verbatim)
"Weeks 1–3: learn. The hackathon brief and team formation open day one, followed by six 60-minute workshops every Monday and Wednesday at 15:00 UTC."
"Weeks 4–6: build. Build your project on Concordium testnet, with Concordium office hours every Tuesday and Friday."
"End of week 6: judging. Submissions close on Wednesday 9 December at 23:59 UTC, and Encode and Concordium then review every demo."

## What you'll build with (verbatim)
"x402: let your agent pay and get paid by other agents, with sponsored transactions so your users never deal with fees."
"ERC-8004: register your agent in the Agent Registry and link the address it already has, on Ethereum, Solana or one of over 200 supported chains. Its Agent Card follows the ERC-8004 format and carries the Verified by Concordium badge."
"Verified by Concordium badge: compatible with over 200 chains, it shows a verified human stands behind your agent. Zero-knowledge proofs keep their personal data private."
"Accountability: keep a record of what your agent does, built to support EU AI Act record keeping."
"Your existing framework: a hosted MCP server and A2A support mean you add all of this without rebuilding your agent."

## What we're looking for (verbatim)
"Test tokens are free, so judging is not about transaction counts. We're looking for depth: how meaningfully your project uses Concordium identity, the Agent Registry and agent payments, and how credible its path to production (going live on mainnet) is."

## The Brief (verbatim)
"🛡️ Make your Agents Accountable with Concordium"
"Take an AI agent you already run, on Ethereum, Solana or one of over 200 supported chains, and make it accountable. It should be able to prove that a verified human stands behind it, pay and get paid on its own, and leave a record. Build it on Concordium testnet, using the tools covered in the six workshops."

### What to build — "directions" (verbatim), explicitly NOT called tracks
"Here are some directions to get you started. They're starting points, not tracks:"
- "Your existing agent: add the Verified by Concordium badge, ERC-8004 and x402 to the agent you already run, on Ethereum, Solana or one of over 200 supported chains."
- "Verified agents: register your agent in the Agent Registry, publish its Agent Card and have other agents check its Verified by Concordium badge before they deal with it, over MCP or A2A."
- "Private, verified users: gate a service on a proven attribute, such as age, with Concordium ID and Verify and Access, without storing anyone's personal data."
- "Agent commerce: agents that pay each other for data, APIs or work using x402, with sponsored transactions so end users never handle fees."
- "Accountable agents: audit trails that support EU AI Act record keeping (Article 12), with a lawful route from an agent back to an accountable owner."

## Eligibility / prior-work question
The page does NOT contain any rule stating that code or projects started before the hackathon are disallowed. There is no "prior work" restriction and no "no projects started before" clause. To the contrary, the brief is framed around existing work:
- "Take an AI agent you already run… and make it accountable."
- "Your existing agent: add the Verified by Concordium badge, ERC-8004 and x402 to the agent you already run…"
- FAQ "Can I bring an agent that lives on another chain?" → "Yes, that is the point. Bring the agent you already run on Ethereum, Solana or one of over 200 supported chains. You are adding accountability to it, not rebuilding it."
So bringing an existing (pre-hackathon) agent is not only allowed, it is the stated intent. The only substantive restriction is the testnet requirement plus a meaningful Concordium integration (below).
